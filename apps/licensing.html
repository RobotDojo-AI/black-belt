Black Belt — Elastic License 2.0

Black Belt — the paid layer that knows your world — is source-available, not open source. The code is public so you can audit it; it runs on a subscription. While your subscription is active, Black Belt unlocks the premium engines plus relay-based remote access. If your subscription lapses, the Black Belt engines pause; your data and prior artifacts are preserved on your machine. Resubscribe to resume.

You may read, inspect, and audit the Black Belt source. You may not run Black Belt without an active subscription, redistribute or resell it, or circumvent the entitlement mechanism.
